# Worldbuilding — Teyvat After the Archons

## Lore

Teyvat is a world of seven nations, each ruled by an Archon — a god who once guided their people. But something changed. The Archons withdrew. Or were sealed away. No one remembers exactly when.

What remains is a world where humans must survive on their own, relying on remnants of divine power, ancient technology, and the elemental forces that still linger in the land.

## The Setting

- **Mondstadt-style freedom lands** — open plains, wind-worn cities, and wandering bards.
- **Liyue-style harbors** — bustling markets where the currency is favors, not gold.
- **Inazuma-style isolation** — islands cut off by eternal storms, hiding secrets behind closed gates.
- **Sumeru-style academies** — libraries that hold more questions than answers.

## The Gacha System

In this world, the Gacha is not a game — it is a force of nature. Characters, weapons, and relics manifest from thin air when certain conditions are met. Some people can trigger them intentionally. Others are born into them.

The protagonist of this story has been here since the beginning. They already know the mechanics. They already have the power. But power without purpose is just noise.

## Rules of the World

1. **Nothing is free.** Every wish has a cost — memory, time, or a piece of your soul.
2. **The stars lie.** Constellations shift to reward those who gamble. Trust nothing above you.
3. **Level 60 is the floor, not the ceiling.** The protagonist is strong, but Teyvat has depths even they have not reached.
4. **Story is the real gacha.** Every chapter pulls a new revelation from the void.
